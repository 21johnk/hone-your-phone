Guide: Hone Your Phone

#CuttheClutter | **Android only for now** Why ↗ Full Digital Detox ↗

Hello, friend!

Thanks for this opportunity to work on your phone. Let’s make it more human.

Let’s cut down on sensory overwhelm. You can skip any step below, or use ▶️ to read the instructions.

1. Turn your phone to grayscale & enable dark mode
  1. Go to Settings → Accessibility → Color correction
  1. Use Color Correction : toggle ‘on’
  1. Select Grayscale (under Intensity )

If you’re not already using dark mode, set that up too:

  1. Go to Settings → Display
  1. Dark theme : toggle ‘on’
2. Disable app icons & set your wallpaper to black
  1. Using your phone, go here: https://f-droid.org/repo/io.github.luantak.fokuslauncher_53.apk

    Fokus Launcher is a free, open-source tool that will disable app icons and set your wallpaper to black. It does not contain malware, and the entire codebase is available online.

  1. After some unnecessary Google-imposed steps, you should be able to go through install and set-up.

If you’re just setting your wallpaper to black:

Touch and hold a blank area on your phone’s home screen, then select Wallpaper. You should be able to set your wallpaper to black from there!

3. Stop all banner notifications

Most people will skip this step. (Stopping banner notifications is mostly covered in the next step.)

If you are a developer, connect your phone to a computer with adb, open adb’s containing folder in your terminal and run this:

.\adb shell settings put global heads_up_notifications_enabled 0

If you implement this, you will need to swipe down from the top of the phone to see the ‘answer call’ button; it will not automatically appear in a banner notification.

4. Disable all notifications for most apps
  1. Go to Settings → Notifications → App notifications.
  1. In the dropdown menu, select All apps (not just Most recent).
  1. Toggle everything to ‘off’, except Phone and Clock.
5. Turn off touch vibration
  1. Go to Settings → Sound & vibration, then scroll down and select Vibration & haptics.
  1. Slide Touch feedback and Media vibration all the way down (= to the left).

If this doesn’t completely work and you are a developer (see above), you can run:

adb shell settings put system haptic_feedback_enabled 0
adb shell settings put system touch_vibration_enabled 0
6. Stop all but the most extreme emergency alerts
  1. Go to Settings → Safety & emergency and scroll down to Wireless emergency alerts.
  1. Toggle every individual option ‘off’, except Extreme threats.
7. Turn off vibration for texts

If you have text notifications enabled (= you did not turn them off in a previous step),

  1. Go to Settings → Notifications → App notifications.
  1. In the dropdown menu, select All apps (not just Most recent).
  1. Scroll down to Messages.
  1. Expand (⌄) Show unused categories.
  1. Select Incoming messages ; on the following screen, make sure Vibration is toggled ‘off’.
8. Turn off notifications on lock screen
  1. Go to Settings → Home & lock screen → Lock screen
  1. Touch Privacy
  1. Select Don't show notifications at all

Let’s also cut down on clutter and distraction.

  1. Choose which apps you want to keep for now & delete the rest

    Philosophy: As Mark Bittman says about kitchens, start with very minimal tools and build from there; you will discover what you actually need.

  1. Choose which apps you need access to all the time Coming soon!
    1. Place some of these on your home screen
    1. Place the rest on the app list (they will appear there by default)
  1. Make the following app categories: Coming soon!
    1. sock drawer (rarely used apps; unhide if you need them)
    1. other ‘unhide drawers’ (apps to use for specific purposes, e.g., work, travel)
    1. disabled, need permission—this is mainly Play Store and browser

Finally, let’s set a time every day for you to not use your phone. For many people, that’s the morning.

  1. You’ll need a separate alarm clock from your phone.
  1. You might also like a CD player or other device for some morning meditation.
